Spec sheet

Old outside, current inside

The shells are new tooling, not salvaged cases. Here is what goes in them.

340mm

Full-length GPUs

Triple-slot cards up to 340 mm, with a vertical mount bracket in the box.

360mm

Radiator in the roof

The top panel lifts off. Room for a 360 mm AIO or three 120 mm fans behind the vents.

USB-C

Hidden I/O

Front USB-C 20 Gbps and two USB-A ports sit behind the floppy-slot flip door.

ABS+PC

UV-stable plastic

Our putty blend resists yellowing. It stays beige on purpose and won't drift to banana.

Will it fit my hardware?

Tower shells take ATX and mATX boards, graphics cards up to 340 mm and a 360 mm radiator in the roof. The Desktop Slab takes mATX or ITX with an SFX power supply. Bay covers fit any standard 5.25" or 3.5" drive bay. The full list is in the spec sheet.
